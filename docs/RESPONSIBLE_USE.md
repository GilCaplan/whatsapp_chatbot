# Using Doppel responsibly

Doppel lets an AI persona chat from **your** WhatsApp account. That's a lot of fun with friends who are
in on the joke, and a real way to hurt people if it isn't. A few ground rules:

## Keep it kind

- **Use it with people who'd laugh about it afterwards.** Pranks among friends, a group game, testing
  a character you're writing: great. Deceiving someone vulnerable, a partner, a colleague or a stranger: not okay.
- **End it well.** Use the reveal message when the joke has run its course, and stop if someone seems
  confused, upset or emotionally invested.
- **Never use it for** scams, harassment, romance or relationship deception, impersonating someone else,
  collecting personal information, or anything you'd be uncomfortable explaining to the people involved.

## Stay in control

- Turn on **Approve before sending** for anyone you don't know well; you see every reply first.
- Keep **hand-off** protections on so serious topics (money, health, meeting up, distress) come back to you.
- Personas speak *as you*: you are responsible for what is sent from your account.

## WhatsApp's rules

Doppel is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta. Automated messaging can break
[WhatsApp's Terms of Service](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/terms-of-service) and may get an account
restricted or banned. Keep volume low, avoid unsolicited messages (leave "Start conversations" off unless you
really want it), and use the realistic timing presets rather than instant replies everywhere.

## Privacy of the people you chat with

Conversations in assigned chats are stored on your computer and, if you pick Claude or OpenAI, sent to that
provider to write replies. Use the local Ollama option for maximum privacy, and clear a chat's history
from its panel when you're done. See [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) for exactly what is stored where.
